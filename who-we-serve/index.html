Organizations whose authority depends on structure.

01 Government and Public Institutions

Public programs, public records, public accountability.

Marchitects supports economic development, civic communications, public programs, accessible digital services, stakeholder engagement, and institutional records.

Public agencies, economic development organizations, special districts, and public programs engage Marchitects when a program needs clear communication, documented accountability, and systems that continue after the launch.

02 Businesses and Growth Organizations

Communication and operations that move together.

Founders, operating teams, and growth-stage companies engage Marchitects when positioning, follow‑up, and daily operations have drifted apart. We connect the message to the workflow so the next action happens reliably.

Engagements have included real estate, storage operations, and technology companies, and the systems behind three companies on the path to public offering.
