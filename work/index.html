Institutional research

California Public Document Audit

Marchitects reviewed the public document archives of 50 California government entities, including agenda archives, planning departments, procurement pages, and public forms.

Challenge
Public archives grow one document at a time, and each untagged PDF repeats the same accessibility defects.
Structure
A review of every archive type across 50 entities, with an exposure rating for each entity and a check of vendor contract scope.
Record
6,294 untagged PDF documents identified. 27 entities rated high PDF exposure. 8 of 11 active vendor contracts carried no PDF remediation in scope.
Applies to
Any organization that publishes documents at volume, including universities, healthcare systems, associations, and regulated businesses.
