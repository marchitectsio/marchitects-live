Notes on the Architecture of Trust.

Working notes from Marchitects on communications, systems, accessibility, and the records institutions use to establish authority.

Compliance
Accessible digital services

Accessibility starts with the structure of the service.

Most accessibility failures in public digital services come from structure: templates without heading order, documents published without tags, and vendor contracts that leave remediation out of scope. Each new page and PDF inherits the defect.

The durable fix is upstream. Define the content structure, the document requirements, the staff workflow, and the vendor acceptance criteria together, and the service stays accessible as it grows.

Trust Systems
Institutional records

Authority is established in the record.

Institutions earn trust through what they can show: the program documented, the decision explained, the result reported. When the record is incomplete, the institution's own account competes with every other version.

A Trust System treats documentation as part of the operating structure, so the record is produced as the work happens.

Trust Systems
Strategic communications
Systems development

Define the language before selecting the tools.

Technology decisions carry the assumptions of the language behind them. When an organization has settled what it says, to whom, and with what evidence, the right workflows, automations, and platforms become easier to choose and easier to adopt.
