Common Questions

Before you schedule.

How do we know what is worth building first?+

Marchitects starts with institutional and business impact. We look for repeatable work, stalled follow‑up, manual intake, underused data, missing documentation, and places where clearer communication can create measurable value.

What happens during the assessment?+

We review your operations, goals, tools, audiences, intake process, follow‑up flow, records, and team capacity. You leave with a prioritized build path.

Can Marchitects build after the assessment?+

Yes. The assessment moves into development across communications, workflow automation, agentic systems, email infrastructure, intake logic, content systems, documentation, and operational tooling.

Do you respond to public solicitations?+

Yes. Public-sector compliance and procurement may be delivered through Marketing Automation Techs LLC when the solicitation requires that entity.

What if we purchased tools before engaging Marchitects?+

Marchitects evaluates what belongs in the operating model, what needs connection, and what can be retired.

Can this work alongside our current CRM, website, or email platform?+

Yes. Marchitects designs around the current environment first, then builds the missing structure around it.
