Institutional logic

Marchitects builds the communications, systems, evidence, documentation, and operating structure institutions use to establish authority and continue functioning over time.

Each public-sector practice applies the Trust Systems method to a specific institutional need. Practices can be engaged separately or combined within one program.

01 Public-Sector Program Architecture

Programs designed to be explained, delivered, and documented.

Program architecture connects a public program's goals to its communication, intake, delivery workflow, and reporting. The structure is defined before launch so the program can show what it did and for whom.

  • Program structure and delivery workflow
  • Intake, eligibility, and participant communication
  • Stakeholder engagement plans
  • Performance reporting and institutional records
02 Civic Communications

Public information residents can find, read, and act on.

Civic communications work gives public institutions a consistent voice across programs, meetings, notices, and digital services. Language, content structure, and accessibility requirements are planned together.

  • Public information and program communications
  • Community and stakeholder engagement
  • Public meeting, agenda, and notice communications
  • Plain language and content standards
  • Institutional records and public accountability
