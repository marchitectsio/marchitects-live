FAQs

Common questions before you schedule an assessment.

How do we know what is worth building first?

Marchitects starts by identifying where time, money, and capacity are being lost. We map the highest-leverage opportunities and define the build sequence that creates measurable value.

What happens during the assessment?

We review your operations, growth goals, current tools, customer journey, and team capacity. The result is a clear build path that shows what should be built first.

Can Marchitects build the system after the assessment?

Yes. When the assessment identifies a viable build path, Marchitects can move into implementation.

Do you build funnels?

Marchitects designs the architecture behind conversion. The assessment determines what belongs in the build and where execution should live.

What if we already bought AI tools?

Marchitects helps determine which tools have practical value, where they fit, and what infrastructure is needed to make them useful inside the business.

Can this work with our current CRM, website, or email platform?

Yes. Marchitects designs around your current operating environment wherever possible. The goal is to connect the systems you already use and build the missing infrastructure around them.

How long until we see useful results?

Useful clarity usually appears during the assessment phase. Build and implementation timelines depend on system complexity and team adoption needs.
