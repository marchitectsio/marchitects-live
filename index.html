The Method

The A.B.C. Structure™

Marchitects uses the A.B.C. Structure™ to assess what is costing the business capacity, build the right solution, and commission it into daily operations.

01
Assess.

We examine how the business runs: where leads stall, where work gets repeated, where data sits unused, and where communication breaks down. The result is a clear build sequence with a rationale behind each decision.

02
Build.

We build the workflows, automations, messaging structures, intake logic, and infrastructure surfaced by the assessment. Each build ties to a practical business function.

03
Commission.

We install the build inside the business, connect it to the team's workflow, and support adoption until the process runs without constant intervention.

Common Questions

Before you schedule.

How do we know what is worth building first?+

Marchitects starts with business impact. We look for repeatable work, stalled follow-up, manual intake, underused data, and places where automation or clearer communication can create measurable value.

What happens during the assessment?+

We review your operations, growth goals, tools, customer journey, intake process, follow-up flow, and team capacity. You leave with a prioritized build path.

Can Marchitects build after the assessment?+

Yes. The assessment moves into development across workflow automation, agentic systems, email infrastructure, intake logic, content systems, and operational tooling.

Do you build funnels?+

Marchitects designs the conversion architecture behind the funnel: lead capture, intake logic, automation sequencing, and the language that moves prospects to action. Social Following Studios handles full-service execution when needed.

What if we purchased tools before engaging Marchitects?+

Marchitects evaluates what belongs in the operating model, what needs connection, and what can be retired or ignored.

Can this work alongside our current CRM, website, or email platform?+

Yes. Marchitects designs around the current environment first, then builds the missing structure around it.
